Going to sacred waters

When I go to sacred waters I go with great reverence and respect. The most important thing that has to be understood when working with water is having great respect for the water. In the bush or in the woods it is the same but I will speak on what I am initiated in. I do not want to say what we are supposed to do in the bush or in the woods because I will leave out other things and I do not want to offend the ones who are initiated in working with or in the bush. When going to water we go with great respect. You go dressed in certain colours. The primary colours are the ones that reflect the colour of water. So we are talking of blues and whites in satin fabric. Now why satin? As a fabric it mimics that shimmer of water. So somehow you are adorning yourself in something that honours the way this energy looks. We barely wear red because red is synonymous with fire and earth elements. So we do not want red in these places because it is a frequency that we do not recognise in this space. When you arrive there at the sacred waters, gently and respectfully. You kneel at the water. You have taken off your shoes. You kneel and wait. You have come with your offerings because in nature we always bring offerings, because there are those that dwell within those spaces. So what people most often think when they go to water is that they are taking these offerings for the njuzu or mermaids, because njuzu is one of the beings that love in the water. So when I am taking offerings to the water I usually take white rice and fruit. I do not take sweets or alcohol. If I take alcohol it is the traditional brew. So we see that the offerings differ as well. So what the offerings are there for is to appease the spirit of that place. Now this is where it becomes quantum. The water spirit comes with many micro-organisms and living beings. We have the fish and other beings that live in water, the njuzu or mermaids. We have animals, the crocodiles that dwell within that space as well. So the offering that you are taking is to give to the energies that dwell within the water ecosystem. You leave rice and pour it into the water. What happens is the rice is going to break up. As it breaks the organisms are going to feed on that and the organisms are part of the water world and the water world is encompassed holistically as the being that it is. It is now quantum.

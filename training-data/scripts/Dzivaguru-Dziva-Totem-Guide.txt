Dzivaguru and the Dziva Totem - A Spiritual Lineage Guide

1. Introduction
This guide explores the ancestral and spiritual connection between Dzivaguru, the Great
Pool Spirit, and the Dziva totem lineage. It combines Shona oral traditions, cosmology,
and sacred geography.

2. Who is Dzivaguru?
Dzivaguru (The Great Pool) is a powerful mhondoro spirit in Shona spirituality. He is known
as a rainmaker, healer and divine messenger who resides in sacred pools and rivers. 
Dzivaguru is believed to be a direct servant of Mwari (God), enforcing justice and blessing the land.

3. Who are the Dziva Totem People?
The Dziva totem people are a Shona clan connected to sacred water, rain rituals and
herbal wisdom. Their totem praise poems often include references to rivers, pools and spiritual
purity. They are guardians of ancestral ceremonies involving the Save River and other
sacred water bodies.

4. Spiritual and Ancestral Connection
Dzivaguru is spiritually linked to the Dziva totem as an ancestral rainmaker and guardian.
The Dziva clan see Dzivaguru as a source of spiritual identity, calling him 'baba wedziva'
(father of the sacred pool). This connection reinforces their role as spiritual healers and
custodians of sacred water traditions.

5. Geographic Connection to the Save River
The Save River, flowing through Manicaland and into Mozambique, is a sacred river in
Shona cosmology. Many Dziva totem clans perform rituals along its banks. Dzivaguru is
believed to reside in deep pools connected to this river, symbolising life, fertility and divine
presence.

6. Totem Praise Names (Zvidawo)
Common Dziva totem praise lines include:
- Mvura yakacheka makomo (water which cleanses the mountains)
- Vana Makombe (the Makombe people)
- Vana Save (the Save people)
- Mwana wedziva guru (child of the great pool)
- Vanoyera pamadziva (those who are blessd at the sacred pools)

7. Oral Sayings and Wisdom
Vanadziva havamboneta nemvura. Vane mukadzi mudziva, baba Dzivaguru.
Translation: Dziva people are never dry. Their mother is the pool, their father is Dzivaguru.

8. Totem Lineage Chart
Spiritual Lineage:

1: Mwari (God)
2: Dzivaguru (Mhondoro spirit)
3: Dziva Totem Clans
4: Masvikiro (Spirit mediums)
5: Community guidance, healing and rituals

9. Conclusion
Dzivaguru and the Dziva totem are spiritually intertwined through water, ancestral power and sacred responsibility. 
This link continues to shape Shona identity, spiritual leadership and cultural preservation.

10. Glossary (Shona Terms)
- Mhondoro: Royal ancestral spirit
- Dziva: Pool / Sacred water
- Totem: Clan identity
- Mwari: God
- Save: Major river in Zimbabwe
- Masvikiro: Spirit mediums

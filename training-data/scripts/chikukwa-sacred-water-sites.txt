SACRED WATER SITES INDIGENOUS KNOWLEDGE AND MYTHOLOGIES

Dziya reMvimvana
It is a sacred pool where the elder women of the community, those who have sacred ancestral knowledge, go to perform the ritual that clears the mvimvana worm or pest from the crops and fields.

Dziya rePwaziya
It is a sacred pool on top of the mountain which was formed by the mermaids as they swallowed the whole Pwaziya family. This happened when the husband disobeyed the natural laws of the water.

Dziya reShato
It is a pool for the python snake. Since the pythons reside there it was named after them.

Dziya raTsangu
Tsangu means seed. So during the ancient times our ancestors used to give offerings of seeds to the water spirits before planting in the fields and after harvesting.

Mutsarara waBarara
It is a place of cleansing after you have been tried at the Chief's place and found guilty of witchcraft. You will be sent to 3 traditional healers or Nanga for confirmation regarding the case so as not to continue as a witch. Then the cleansing process will be done by the community elders and healers at this waterfall.

Mutsarara waMuoha (Mozambique)
A place for the mermaids where they stay and commune. It is a very powerful space.

Mutsarara waMuohwa (Zimbabwe)
A place for mermaids where they stay and commune. It is a very powerful space.

Rwizi rwaMusapa (upstream by the school, and downstream into Mozambique)
A river that has many sacred dams and should not be polluted, no matter what is happening in the life of humans. It should remain clean and pure as it is a dwelling of the water beings of the land, water and cosmos.

Tsime raManhenge
A spring where before activities are done, like working in the wetlands, offerings and rituals are given by the elder women of the Ungweme tribe. These offerings are done every year to inform and seek protection from the ancestors for pests and diseases that affect our crops. 

Tsime Ndamuka
A spring that needs to be kept clean and pure from any human dirt/ chemicals/ waste/ pollution as water beings do not like dirt. 

Tsime Muzuru
A spring that needs to be kept clean and pure from any human dirt/ chemicals/ waste/ pollution as water beings do not like dirt.

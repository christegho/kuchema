IKS for combatting pests

Testimony:
Gore rapera, kwakabuda mvimvana, rakaita muno maChikukwa. Rakaita mumunda mangu. Ndakaenda kwaMambk kunotaura. Ndaka ndamboda kubata godjo ndakuspreya. Pane akawuya achiti aiwa musaspreye. Zvamunoita, endai munotaura kwaMambo kuti ndo zvamasangana nazvo. Ndakabva ndaenda ndabva ndataura kuvasvaruka venzvimbo ino. Ndasvika ndikabva ndataura zvandaitaura. Vakati imi chinyararai muchazowona zvichaitika. Vakatuma vanhu vavakatuma, maAdziTete vemuno munzvimbo ino. Madzitete enzvimbo ino. Vakangoti kuti muchazowona vanhu vacha svika. Ini vakazo bvunza vasvika vachiti zviri nekupi, ndikati kwakadai kwakadai. Apo here? Vakati chidzokera kumba kwako. Ndobva ndadzokera. Zvakazoitwa ikoko ini handina kuzviwona. Asi ndakazowona vakuenda vachidaidzira zvavaidaidzira vachienda. Zveshuwa after a week manga musisina kana rimwe. /

Last year, mvimvana (worms/ pests) appeared here in Chikukwa. They appeared in my field. I went to the Mambo, the Chief, to report it. At first, I had wanted to get the pesticide and spray them. But someone came and sai: No, do not spray them. What you should do is go and tell the Chief what you have encountered.
 
So I went and spoke to the elders of this area. When I arrived, I explained what had happened. They said: You, just keep quiet. You will see what happens.

They sent the people they needed to send - the Madzitete of this area, the aunts/ female elders of this place.
They simply said: You will see some people arriving.

When those people arrived, they asked me: Where exactly is it?
I told them where it was: There, in that place.

Then they told me: Go back to your home.

So I went home.

I did not see what they did there. But afterwards, I saw them leaving, calling out or chanting whatever it was that they were calling out, as they went. And truly, after a week, there was not a single worm left in the crop.

Matateguru vanoenda nemvimvana kumvura. / The elder women take the mvimvana (worm/ pest) to the water.

On the sacredness of rituals: 
Kana ichinzi iritual, pane zvinenge zvichiereswa kuti zvisa wonekwa. Kuti zvinoitwa kudziya kuti uzvizive, ndo kutoera kwazvo haufanirwe ku zviziva. Zvino zikanwa nanambuya vanobata muromo. / 

When something is called a ritual, there are certain things that are kept sacred and are not supposed to be seen. If what is done were explained to you so that you could know exactly what happens, then it would no longer retain its sacredness. Its sacredness lies precisely in the fact that you are not supposed to know.

Those who do know, the grandmothers/ elder women (vanambuya), keep their mouths closed. They do not speak about it.
